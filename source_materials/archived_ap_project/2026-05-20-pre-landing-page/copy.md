# Reusable Landing Page Copy Framework

This file is a fill-in framework for the future customer/coaching offer.

## Source-First Copy Workflow

1. Collect source material:
   - Coach website
   - Existing sales page
   - Social posts
   - Emails
   - Testimonials
   - Call transcripts
   - Program notes
   - Calendar/booking copy
   - Brand guide

2. Extract:
   - Audience language
   - Pain points
   - Desired outcomes
   - Offer mechanism
   - Coach credibility
   - Objections
   - Proof
   - CTA path

3. Build voice profile:
   - Sentence rhythm
   - Common phrases
   - Level of warmth
   - Directness
   - Words to use
   - Words to avoid

4. Draft page:
   - Use one core message
   - Keep one primary CTA
   - Tie every section to conversion

## Hero

Eyebrow:
[Offer category or audience]

Headline options:
- [Outcome] without [pain]
- [Offer name] for [specific audience]
- Build [desired future state] with [method]
- Stop [specific struggle]. Start [specific result].

Subheadline:
[Coach/company] helps [audience] go from [current problem] to [desired outcome] through [method/program format].

Primary CTA:
[Book a Call / Apply Now / Reserve Your Spot / Start Here]

CTA microcopy:
[Takes 2 minutes / Opens calendar / No payment required / Limited spots]

## Three-Coach Hero Option

Use this when the landing page should immediately communicate that visitors can choose from three integration coaches.

Eyebrow:
[Integration coaching / Coaching team / Post-experience support]

Headline:
[Outcome] with the right coach for your integration journey

Subheadline:
Meet three experienced coaches who help [audience] move from [current state] to [desired outcome] through [method/support format].

Primary CTA:
[Book an Integration Call]

Secondary CTA:
[Meet the Coaches]

Hero visual:
- Option A: three coach portraits in a balanced layout.
- Option B: one warm program/retreat image with coach portraits introduced in the next section.
- Option C: one group photo if the final assets include one.

## Pain Section

Headline:
If [current situation] feels like [specific pain], this was built for you.

Body prompts:
- What is the visitor dealing with right now?
- What have they already tried?
- Why has that not worked?
- What is the emotional cost?
- What is the practical cost?

Bullets:
- You are [specific symptom].
- You have tried [common solution], but [problem remains].
- You want [outcome], but [obstacle].
- You are tired of [frustrating cycle].

## Why This Matters

Headline:
The longer [problem] continues, the more [cost] compounds.

Body:
Explain the stakes. Keep it grounded. Do not exaggerate. Connect the problem to the need for coaching, structure, accountability, support, or a proven method.

## Who It Is For

Headline:
This is for you if...

Bullets:
- You are [audience segment].
- You want [specific outcome].
- You are ready for [level of effort].
- You value [coaching style or method].
- You need [support / clarity / accountability / structure].

Optional not-for-you:
- This is not for you if you want [misaligned expectation].
- This is not a fit if [condition].

## Method / Framework

Headline:
How the process works

Structure options:
- 3 pillars
- 4 phases
- 6-week journey
- Diagnosis -> plan -> implementation -> review
- Clarity -> practice -> feedback -> accountability

For each step:
- Name
- Purpose
- What happens
- What the client walks away with

## Outcomes

Headline:
What you will be able to do after this

Outcome card format:
- Outcome: [specific capability]
- Why it matters: [practical benefit]
- How it happens: [method/tool/support]

Good outcomes are:
- Believable
- Specific
- Tied to the offer mechanism
- Not framed as guaranteed unless legally approved

## What Is Included

Headline:
What you get

Items:
- [Number] live sessions
- [Duration] per session
- [Support channel]
- [Templates/resources]
- [Personalized plan]
- [Community access]
- [Recordings]
- [Follow-up support]

## Coach Bio

Headline:
Meet [coach name]

Bio structure:
- Role and specialty
- Relevant credential or lived experience
- Who they help
- Method or belief
- Why clients trust them

Avoid:
- Long autobiography
- Generic passion statements
- Credentials that do not matter to the visitor

## Coach Team Section

Use three repeated coach profiles. Keep each card consistent so visitors can compare quickly.

Coach profile fields:
- Name: [coach name]
- Role: [coach role/specialty]
- Credentials: [credential or relevant lived/professional experience]
- Best fit: [who should book with this coach]
- Bio: [80-120 words]
- Method: [coaching approach/framework]
- Calendar CTA: [Book with first name]
- Calendar URL: [coach-specific calendar link]

Coach section headline:
Choose the support that fits where you are now.

Coach section body:
Each coach brings a different lens to integration. Read the profiles below, then book the conversation that feels most aligned.

Required final source material:
- Three final photos
- Three approved bios
- Three calendar URLs or one confirmed shared team calendar

## Proof

Testimonial structure:
- Before: what was the client struggling with?
- Experience: what was different about the process?
- After: what changed?
- Specificity: numbers, decisions, clarity, behavior, confidence, results

Proof section options:
- Short quote grid
- One featured story
- Results bullets
- Screenshots
- Logos
- Ratings/reviews

## Pricing / Booking

Headline:
Start with [offer name]

If price is public:
- Price
- What is included
- Payment options
- Refund/guarantee note
- CTA to checkout or calendar

If price is call-based:
- Explain why a call is required
- Tell them what happens on the call
- Reassure no-pressure if true
- CTA to calendar

## FAQ

Starter questions:
- Who is this for?
- What happens after I book?
- How does the coaching work?
- How long is the program?
- Is it live?
- What if I miss a session?
- Do I need experience before joining?
- What results are realistic?
- Is there support between sessions?
- How do I know if it is a fit?

## Final CTA

Headline:
Ready to [desired outcome]?

Body:
If you are [audience] and want [outcome], the next step is [calendar / application / checkout].

CTA:
[Book a Call / Apply Now / Reserve Your Spot]

Fallback:
Questions? Contact [email / WhatsApp / form].
